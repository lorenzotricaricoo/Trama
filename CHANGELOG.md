# Changelog

All notable changes to Trama are listed here. Versions follow
[Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/); the notes are written from the
[Conventional Commits](https://www.conventionalcommits.org/) merged since the previous
release ([releasing](docs/development/releasing.md)).

## [0.1.0-alpha.1] - 2026-11-28

First pre-release of phase 1, for trying Trama on real meetings. It is not notarized:
see [Install](README.md#install) for the first launch. Search, the Home, onboarding,
the menu bar and audio retention are not there yet.

### Added

- **Meeting recording** on two aligned tracks, the microphone and the system audio, with
  a recording window showing the timer, the levels and notes taken during the call
  ([#31](https://github.com/lorenzotricaricoo/Trama/issues/31)).
- **Local transcription** of both tracks into one timeline, in the background with
  progress; the model (about 2.5 GB) is downloaded from the settings, with resume and
  checksum.
- **Title and summary** generated by the AI provider chosen for the task, with key
  points tied to the moment they were said.
- **Meeting page**: the summary and its key points, editable; the transcript; a player
  for both tracks that jumps to a key point or a line; rename, copy the summary, retry
  a failed transcription ([#33](https://github.com/lorenzotricaricoo/Trama/issues/33)).
- **Markdown notes** with automatic saving.
- **Settings**: language, appearance, recording devices, transcription model, AI
  providers (keys kept in the macOS Keychain) and data.
- **App shell** with sidebar and command bar, in Italian and English.
